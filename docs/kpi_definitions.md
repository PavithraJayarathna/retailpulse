# RetailPulse — KPI Definitions

## 1. Purpose

This document defines the core business metrics used throughout RetailPulse.

The same definitions should be used across SQL queries, Python analysis,
machine learning features, and BI dashboards to maintain consistent reporting.

---

## 2. Gross Sales

### Definition
Total merchandise value before discounts, returns, and refunds.

### Formula
Gross Sales = Σ(Unit Price × Quantity)

### Grain
Calculated from individual order lines and aggregated to the required reporting level.

### Business Use
Measures the total value of merchandise ordered before commercial adjustments.

---

## 3. Discount Amount

### Definition
Total discount applied to qualifying order lines.

### Formula
Discount Amount = Σ(Line Discount)

### Business Use
Measures the financial value of discounts provided to customers.

---

## 4. Net Revenue Before Returns

### Definition
Gross sales after discounts but before return/refund adjustments.

### Formula
Net Revenue Before Returns = Gross Sales - Discount Amount

### Business Use
Measures sales value after pricing discounts.

### Important Note
Returns and refunds will be handled separately once the return and payment
event models are finalized.

---

## 5. Cost of Goods Sold (COGS)

### Definition
Direct product cost associated with sold quantities.

### Formula
COGS = Σ(Unit Cost × Quantity)

### Business Use
Represents the direct merchandise cost associated with sales.

---

## 6. Gross Profit Before Returns

### Formula
Gross Profit Before Returns =
Net Revenue Before Returns - COGS

### Business Use
Measures merchandise profitability before return/refund adjustments.

---

## 7. Gross Margin Percentage

### Formula
Gross Margin % =
(Gross Profit Before Returns / Net Revenue Before Returns) × 100

### Edge Case
If Net Revenue Before Returns is zero, the metric should return NULL rather
than divide by zero.

---

## 8. Average Order Value (AOV)

### Formula
AOV =
Net Revenue Before Returns / Number of Qualifying Orders

### Business Use
Measures the average sales value generated by an order.

### Important Note
The exact definition of a qualifying order will be finalized when order
statuses are defined.

---

## 9. Repeat Purchase Rate

### Definition
Percentage of purchasing customers who have more than one qualifying order.

### Formula
Repeat Purchase Rate =
(Customers with More Than One Qualifying Order / Purchasing Customers) × 100

### Business Use
Provides an indicator of repeat purchasing behaviour.

---

## 10. Return Rate

### Initial Definition
Percentage of sold item quantity that is subsequently returned.

### Formula
Return Rate =
(Returned Quantity / Sold Quantity) × 100

### Business Use
Helps identify products, categories, or operational areas with unusually
high return behaviour.

---

## 11. RFM Metrics

### Recency
Number of days between the analysis reference date and a customer's most
recent qualifying purchase.

### Frequency
Number of qualifying purchases made by a customer during the analysis period.

### Monetary
Customer monetary value during the analysis period.

The exact monetary measure used for segmentation will be evaluated during
the analytical phase.

---

## 12. Customer Lifetime Value

Customer Lifetime Value will not initially be represented using a simplified
fixed formula.

RetailPulse will first measure historical customer value using revenue,
profit, frequency, and purchasing behaviour.

A predictive CLV model may later be developed during the data science phase
if the available data supports reliable estimation.

---

## 13. Metrics Requiring Final Business Rules

The following definitions will be finalized after source data profiling:

- Qualifying order statuses
- Cancelled order treatment
- Partial returns
- Full returns
- Refunds
- Failed payments
- Shipping charges
- Taxes
- Promotional discounts
- Multi-currency conversion
